The Hall of Shame

Six made-up colleges, each with the red flags real students should watch for. Any resemblance to a real institution is a coincidence.

Mirage Institute of Technology

Worst #1

Brochure shows a glass campus. The real one is a rented floor above a tyre shop.

  • Labs exist only in photos
  • “Industry visit” = a bus ride
Fees: ₹3,50,000/yrWarn others

Placement-Promise Polytechnic

Worst #2

100% placement claimed, but the “company” is the owner’s cousin.

  • Salary details never shown
  • Alumni can’t be found
Fees: ₹2,80,000/yrWarn others

Attendance-Only Arts College

Worst #3

Teaching is optional. Attendance shortage fines are not.

  • Syllabus 10 years old
  • Library locked on weekdays
Fees: ₹1,20,000/yrWarn others

Hidden-Fee Engineering College

Worst #4

Quoted fee is a trailer. Lab, exam, bus and “development” fees make the film.

  • No written fee breakdown
  • Receipts handwritten
Fees: ₹4,00,000+/yrWarn others

No-Approval Management School

Worst #5

Degree looks grand until you learn the university never recognised it.

  • No AICTE / UGC listing
  • Refuses to show affiliation
Fees: ₹2,00,000/yrWarn others

Faculty-Wanted Science College

Worst #6

Five departments, two teachers. The physics professor also runs the canteen.

  • Students teach juniors
  • Exams postponed monthly
Fees: ₹90,000/yrWarn others

How to Spot a Bad College

Visit in person

Walk the labs, hostel and library yourself. Photos lie.

Verify approvals

Check university and regulator websites, not the brochure.

Ask alumni

Seniors from the last three batches will tell you the truth.

Get fees in writing

Ask for the full four-year cost before paying a rupee.

Know a college that cheated students?
